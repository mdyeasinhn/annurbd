Welcome to Annur Group's Islamic Madrasa


At Annur Group's Islamic Madrasa, we are dedicated to providing a nurturing environment where students grow spiritually, intellectually, and morally. Rooted in Islamic values, our madrasa offers a comprehensive education that combines traditional Islamic teachings with modern academic learning.

Our Mission


To educate and empower students to become responsible, ethical, and knowledgeable individuals who contribute positively to society, while adhering to the principles of Islam.

What We Offer


  • Islamic Education: Quranic studies, Hadith, Fiqh, Tafsir, and Islamic History.
  • Academic Curriculum: Subjects including Mathematics, Science, English, and Social Studies, integrated with Islamic values.
  • Character Building: We emphasize the importance of discipline, respect, and compassion, ensuring students develop strong moral character.
  • Personalized Learning: Individual attention for each student, focusing on their unique needs and academic growth.

Why Choose Our Madrasa?


  • Qualified Teachers: Our experienced and qualified teachers are passionate about both Islamic education and general studies.
  • Holistic Development: We focus on both academic excellence and spiritual growth, offering a balanced approach to education.
  • Safe & Supportive Environment: A welcoming and secure atmosphere where students can thrive academically and spiritually.
  • Community Engagement: We encourage students to participate in community services, helping them develop empathy and responsibility.
