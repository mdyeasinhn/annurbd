Annur Group: Export-Import Services


Annur Group specializes in providing efficient and reliable export-import services, connecting Bangladesh to global markets. Our experienced team ensures smooth operations for businesses looking to expand or source products internationally.

Our Services

  • Import: Documentation, customs clearance, freight forwarding, and timely delivery.
  • Logistics: Cargo tracking, warehousing, and supply chain management.
